This development website introduces 14th APBiomech and the information being prepared for the conference community.

The conference will take place in Bali, Indonesia. The host institution is Institut Teknologi Bandung, through the Faculty of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering.

Abstract submission and registration are not yet open. The Organizing Committee will publish approved deadlines, guidelines, fees, and portal links here when they are available.

This is a website preview notice, not an announcement that the official conference website has launched.
